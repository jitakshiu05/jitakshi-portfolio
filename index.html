About

My journey

I studied psychology because I was curious about what makes people tick, and I ended up in recruiting because it's where that curiosity does the most good.

My path started on the shop floor. As a Sales Lead at Aldo while studying at Douglas College, I learned that great service is mostly about listening. Our store grew revenue by 15%, foot traffic by 10%, and held customer satisfaction at 98%.

That instinct carried me into my first recruiting role at HTA Products, where I ran the full hiring cycle for three years and helped shape the interview frameworks the team used to assess candidates more consistently. Leading marketing coordination at Next Step Marketing then taught me how brand and storytelling move people; the campaigns I supported lifted brand awareness by 15%.

After finishing my BA in Psychology at Simon Fraser University, I moved to Seattle and into tech. At Scale Without Borders I sourced and hired software engineers at every level. At Amazon I learned to coordinate at real scale. At Databricks I supported university and experienced hiring for Product and Engineering, including sensitive senior-level loops.

Today I'm the first Recruiting Coordinator in the Americas at Nscale. My job is no longer only to run the process. It's to design it.
